    How the calculator works

    Each month, interest is added to every debt (balance x APR / 12), then every debt gets its minimum payment. Your extra payment, plus the minimums freed up by debts you have already cleared, goes to one target debt: the smallest balance for the snowball method, the highest APR for the avalanche method. When the target is paid off, the next one takes its place, so the payment keeps growing like a snowball. Avalanche usually saves a little more interest; snowball gives quicker wins. The comparison table shows both for your numbers.

    Assumptions: fixed APRs, payments made once a month, no new charges, no fees. Results are estimates for planning, not financial advice.
