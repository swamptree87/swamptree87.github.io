How the assets are made

Every sky is rendered as a function of the 3D view direction, so there is no pole pinching and cube faces join without seams. The materials are generated procedurally and checked numerically for tiling. The generators are AI-assisted and the results are reviewed and tuned by hand. Every store listing carries an AI-content disclosure.
